Endless Backrooms: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Endless Backrooms has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or location. Your settings and whether you bought REMOVE ADS are saved only on your device.

Ads

The free mobile version shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and basic device and usage data. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Purchases

The REMOVE ADS purchase is handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details; the store tells the game only that you own it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.
